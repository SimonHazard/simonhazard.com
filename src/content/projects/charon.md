Charon v0.1.0 is available in early access. Capture ideas, organize notes with tags and attachments, and copy Markdown context for AI agents. Your files stay in a local folder you control, with no account or sync.

Selected-text capture is available on macOS 14 and newer. Manual entry is available on macOS, Windows, and Linux.
